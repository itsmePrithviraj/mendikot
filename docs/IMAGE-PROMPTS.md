# Mendikot reference-image prompts

## Game description

Mendikot is a four-player Indian partnership trick-taking card game. Partners sit opposite each other and try to capture the four 10s. Follow the suit led whenever possible; hukum is the trump suit. A complete deal has 13 tricks.

## Shared visual direction

Design a warm, tactile modern card game called Mendikot. Deep forest-green felt, ivory playing cards, ink-black spades/clubs, oxblood hearts/diamonds, and restrained brass details. Clear typography, generous playable card targets, a quiet premium tabletop feel rather than casino clutter. A small sheep illustration decorates the 10s; preserve the numeral 10 and suit in both corners. Never put the word TEN on a card. Symmetrical forest-green and ivory card backs with a fine geometric woven pattern and small sheep medallion. Four player positions, partner opposite, central trick, hukum indicator, captured-ten score. No coins, betting, ads or fake chat.
